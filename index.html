The planner that builds itself.

Selfwind Planner learns how you actually spend your time and turns it into a schedule for you. You stop rebuilding your calendar every week, and your plan keeps itself up to date.

What I'm building

Most planners make you do all the work: typing in every task, dragging blocks around, and starting over when your week changes. Selfwind Planner is named after a self-winding watch, which keeps running from your everyday movement. It watches your routines, deadlines, and habits, then builds and adjusts your schedule automatically, so planning takes minutes instead of hours.

1

It learns your rhythm

Tell it about your classes, work, and goals once. It picks up when you focus best and how long things really take.

2

It builds your week

It lays out a realistic schedule around your fixed commitments, with time blocked for the work that matters.

3

It adapts as life happens

When something moves or runs long, it reshuffles the rest of your plan for you instead of leaving you to fix it.

Add events without typing

Getting dates into a calendar shouldn't mean typing them in by hand. Selfwind Planner will pull them straight from the videos and photos you already have.

▶

Upload a video

Upload a video or recording, like a class lecture, a meeting, or a coach's announcement. Selfwind Planner listens to the audio, picks out any dates and times that are mentioned, and adds them to your calendar.

▣

Snap a photo

Take a picture of a syllabus, flyer, whiteboard, or printed schedule. Selfwind Planner reads the text in the photo, finds the dates and deadlines, and adds them to your calendar for you.
